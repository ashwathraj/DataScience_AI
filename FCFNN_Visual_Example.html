Fully connected neural network: 5×5 digit classifier

A 25 → 16 → 12 → 10 network, trained live in your browser on tiny 5×5 digit patterns. Pick a digit or click pixels to draw, then watch the image flatten and flow through the layers.

How to read it
  • Purple hidden neurons glow brighter the more strongly they fire; grey means ReLU output 0.
  • Green connections push the next neuron up (positive weight); orange connections push it down (negative weight).
  • Total learnable parameters: (25×16+16) + (16×12+12) + (12×10+10) = 750.
  • Each page load trains a fresh network from random weights, so results can differ slightly.
